## TL;DR — current state

The draft board runs a **3-layer valuation model** (asset → scarcity → auction-$ → market edge → recommendation + confidence), differentiated cleanly from the top of the board to the deepest bench dart. The **historical-usage panel** now backs every drop-down with real share/efficiency evidence, gated trends, and a role-stability input to confidence. The `/waivers` command speaks the same language against the FAAB market (live once the season starts). The **scouting-brief evidence layer** is built and **complete across the 200-player skill pool** (`/scout`, resumable, kept fresh by a twice-daily re-scout drip). The board itself is now a draft-day tool, not just a ranking: a **target rail** with snake-aware "will he last to my pick?", bye columns, and a player search. On draft day it **runs itself** — paste a Sleeper draft ID and it strikes picks off every 10s, raises an **on-deck prep panel** when your turn is ≤3 away, and fills a **roster grid** as your team comes together. For the run-up, **`/deep-scout`** (§2.8) fans the re-scout queue out to parallel agents batched by NFL team so a backfield is resolved as one question, merges through a single writer, and is meant to run **once, two days out** rather than on draft day. Under the rail sits an **offensive-environment layer** (§1.19): all 32 offenses by projected points scored next to how much of that scoring this league's starter shape actually collects, kept as context rather than wired into value. **The 2026 draft is complete** (15 rounds, 150 picks, 2026-08-22), which turns the system's centre of gravity from one roster to ten: the new **roster room** (§1.20) at `/site/rosters.html` is the middle page of three, scouting every team in the league by recomputed optimal lineup, per-slot rank, bench surplus and six-season owner dossier, and proposing only the trades where **both** lineups rise. As of 2026-09-01 three panels sit where they belong: the **standings moved off the front page into the roster room's league table** (sorted on them, falling back to the projection while every record is 0-0), **my roster took the featured slot on HQ**, and a team's roster now opens **inside the league row you click**. Every player name on the site is a control: clicking one opens a **dossier** of the dated events naming that player, the scouting brief with its sources, the ADP read and the availability score (§1.22).

## What's built & live

### Valuation redesign — the 3-layer model (replaces the old VORP-as-value board)
- **① Asset value** = `rate` (Sleeper proj ÷ 17) × **median games** (light playing-time model), floored at 0. "What he produces," position-agnostic, descriptive — not the sort.
- **Scarcity → draft-$**: integer **auction price ≥ $1** above the replacement line; a **sub-$1 proximity score** (`asset ÷ position-replacement`, $0.05–0.99) below it — position-relative, so the bench differentiates without the old cross-positional pile-up. `draftRank` is fully ordered board-wide.
- **③ Market edge** = your draft-$ vs the market-$ at the player's ADP slot → **TARGET / FAIR / FADE**. Above $1 the driver is the $-edge (±$4); below $1 it's the board-vs-ADP **pick-gap** — and because a projection-based board is structurally low on late-round upside, there's **no TARGET below $1**, only **FADE on real overpays**, with **ceiling as the late-round lens**.
- **② Rec-confidence** = `min(edge strength, asset confidence)`, shown as `●●●/●●○/●○○` dots + a cap reason. Asset confidence = `min(playing-time risk, expert/consensus disagreement)`.
- **Ceiling** (spike-week rate, from Sleeper weekly 2023–25) is a **separate display attribute**, never a value input. **Availability** is a light median-games input + the Phase-B uncertainty band (no longer a value multiplier). A **scarcity (replacement-basis) knob** slides starters↔rostered.
- **Board leads with Boris Chen** (2026-07-23 rework): default sort = BC rank, and the columns read BC · **BC−ADP** (spots the market lets him fall past consensus; +blue value / −red reach) · ADP · $val · edge · ceil — the at-a-glance decision row. Column headers **lock to the top on scroll** (sticky beneath the sticky toolbar, offset measured live). Sort toggles: BC / BC−ADP / ADP / $val / edge.
- **Drop-down is prioritized, not exhaustive** (2026-07-27): it opens on the four reads that drive a pick — **Why here** and **Scouting** full-width, then a three-column row of **Historical usage · Trends · Boris Chen** — and puts the rest (Value & $ engine, Ceiling, Availability, Situation & risk, "How this value is built") behind compact disclosure buttons, all collapsed by default. **1577px → 523px** on open, about a third of the original height. Nothing was removed and no black box: every field is one click away, section state is board-wide and survives repaints, and it resets compact on reload. K/DEF/rookies render no Trends column and let usage span the gap.
- **Tiers read as slabs** (2026-08-09): every tiered row carries its Boris Chen tier as a 5px left band in **every sort and view**, and a **full-row wash on the BC sort only** — the wash is a grouping device, so it appears only where the tiers run contiguous (elsewhere it's confetti; the footer legend swaps to match). A color rule caps each block on the BC sort (20 of them; the rule self-disables where tiers interleave, as they do among K/DEF at the tail). Hues step by the **golden angle**, so adjacent tiers measure **ΔE 66–167** on the band against a just-noticeable threshold of ~2.3; a period-3 lightness step pushes the hue cycle's near-repeat out past the end of the board and adds a colorblind-safe second axis. Drafted rows drop the wash and keep the band.

- **Target rail** (2026-08-09): a sticky shortlist column beside the board. ☆ on any row adds a player; the rail sorts by ADP and shows, per target, his ADP + its round and **how far away he is right now** — `29 past ADP` (green: the market is letting him fall), `on the clock`, or `45 picks · 4.5 rd`. Distance counts from a live pick number — Sleeper's actual pick count when a draft is connected (see **Live draft sync** below), falling back to a count of your drafted marks (`taken.size + 1`) when it isn't — and the rail states which derivation it's using rather than hiding it. Targets **drop off when marked drafted** by filtering, not deleting, so a mis-click is reversible and a `N gone:` line still tells you who got sniped. Rail is independent of the board's filters/sort/view; below 1040px it stacks above the board. **Set your draft slot** and it also lists the picks you still hold and answers "does he reach one" per target: `lasts to #38` / `coin flip #38` / `gone by #38`, pivoting to the wheel (`back at #43` / `now or never`) on the tick where the pick is yours. Snake geometry is measured from `draft-meta-2023..25.json` (10 teams, 15 rounds, no reversal round) and validated against the real 2025 picks; the ±5-pick slack is a stated rule of thumb, not a computed probability, and the tooltips say so. A **bye column** sits on each row and turns amber when another live target shares the week (naming them), which is the read a shortlist actually needs.
- **Bye weeks are now on every board player** (2026-08-09), derived at build time from `api.sleeper.app/schedule/nfl/regular/2026` (a team's bye = the week it plays no game), validated 32-teams-one-bye-each before it ships and nulled wholesale on any failure. 2026: 32/32, weeks 5–14. The field is `p.bye`. The **main board** shows it as the last cell of the metric cluster (after `ceil`), with a tooltip naming any targets on the same week; it deliberately carries **no color**, because a bye belongs to the team, so a collision highlight lit 44 of 248 rows and failed the same wallpaper bar the revisit flag was cut against. The rail keeps its amber, where a shared week is 2 rows out of ~8. Mobile cluster `min-width`s were trimmed to absorb the 8th cell, leaving rows shorter at 375px than before the column existed.

- **Live draft sync** (2026-08-13): paste a Sleeper draft ID in the toolbar, hit connect, and the board **marks drafted players off by itself** every 10s — the ✓ column stops being 150 clicks of bookkeeping. Your **draft slot is read from `draft_order`** (dropdown disabled while connected) and the rail's pick number comes from Sleeper's count, retiring the `taken.size + 1` weakness §1.13 documented. **Cache-busting is load-bearing, not hygiene**: measured live, the plain picks URL returned **41 picks while a busted URL returned 65**, then 65 vs 119 — Cloudflare's `stale-while-revalidate=300` serves 80s-old responses, and staleness scales as `cache_age × pick_rate`. Sleeper's CORS is open (`allow-origin: *`, preflight permits `Cache-Control`), so the page calls the API directly with no proxy. Manual marks and Sleeper's picks are **separate sets** unioned for display: the live set is never persisted (no stale mock bleeding into the real draft), a Sleeper-drafted player can't be un-marked, and `reset draft` clears only yours. Refuses rather than guesses on `reversal_round > 0` or a non-10×15-snake shape; errors back off 10s→60s keeping the last good picture; polling stops at `complete`. Verified across **three live mocks** end to end: strike latency median **~900ms** (min 369ms, max 9.2s = one poll cycle, the designed worst case). In a real 30-40s-per-pick draft expect a player struck ~5s after he's taken. Board strikes 148 of 150 — the misses are deep kickers outside the 16-K pool — while the pick *count* stays exact because it reads feed length, not match count. Full spec: `plans/valuation-and-scouting.md` §1.15.
- **On-deck prep** (2026-08-13): with a draft connected, a panel appears in the rail when your pick is **≤3 away** — your roster so far, the **top 6 available across at most 3 positions of need** ranked by Boris Chen, the **tier cliff** behind each, per-candidate survival to your pick, and **run pressure** over the last 10 picks. Survival **pivots to the wheel when you're on the clock** — at pick #2 it reads `now or never` on Chase/Gibbs/Lamb and `back at #19` on Nabers, instead of asking the meaningless "does he last to the pick I'm making right now" (caught live 2026-08-13; §1.13's rail already pivoted, the panel didn't). Fully deterministic (no LLM, no session, works on the deployed dashboard alone) and it **names no single player**: one name dies the instant someone drafts him mid-read, and breadth over a shortlist is the point. Capping the ranked pool is what makes it usable — filtering on "open starting slot" alone put K/DEF in a round-3 panel with a meaningless "1 left in tier 22" cliff, whereas ranking the whole eligible pool drops them on their own merits until they really are the best left (verified at pick 138: `open: K` alone). Hidden when it can't be honest: >3 picks out, no slot, `reversal_round` set, or **disconnected** (without the feed there's no way to know which drafted players are *yours*). **Replay mode** `?at=<pickNo>` truncates a finished draft to that pick so you can rehearse against a real sequence; labelled REPLAY and polling frozen. Spec: §1.16. **Phase 2 (Claude-written judgment layered into the panel) is not built**; the in-page question box was considered and cut.
- **Roster panel** (2026-08-14): a collapsible grid under the prep box — ten starting slots (`QB RB RB WR WR TE FLEX FLEX K DEF`), five bench, filled from the live pick feed as picks land. **Live-feed only by construction**: a ✓ says a player is *gone*, never that *you* took him, so unconnected it states that instead of guessing a roster from your marks. Roster shape is now **one shared function** — the prep panel's "what's still open" is derived from the same `assignLineup()` the grid renders, so the two can't contradict each other on screen (sharing it immediately exposed a miscount: `5 starting slots` printed above six empty ones, because the count was of distinct positions; now counted, `RB, WR×2`). Off-board picks still render from Sleeper's own record, flagged `*` with **no bye and no valuation** rather than invented ones (2 of 150 in the test draft, both deep kickers). **Bye stacks among starters** are called out — five bench spots don't cover two starters off the same week. During the prep window the grid caps at 26vh so the target list can't be crushed to a sliver; outside it, all 15 rows show whole. Verified via replay against completed mock `1393658399429754880` (seat 2): 15/15, exact snake `1.02 … 15.02`, `bye stack: W7 ×2 · W8 ×2 · W13 ×3`. Spec: §1.17.
- **Rookies filter** (2026-08-14): a `rookies` button right of DEF — 20 first-year players across every position, for spending a last pick or two on a flier. The filter on its own would have been close to useless, which is the interesting part: **no rookie has a Boris Chen rank** (so the default BC sort leaves the view in arbitrary order — the board now says so, and only while that sort is active) and **none has ceiling data** (spike-week rate needs 10+ NFL games, so the one column that answers "high ceiling?" is blank for exactly these players). The fix was to surface what the board does hold — **NFL draft capital** from `context.rookie_capital`, previously rendered nowhere — as an `R1.03` badge on every rookie, labelled evidence next to the scouting brief and explicitly **not** a projection. Verified at replay pick 130 with hide-drafted on: 15 rookies left, all badged, note recounting to 15, e.g. Fernando Mendoza (`R1.01`, the #1 overall pick) sitting at ADP 199.6. Spec: §1.18.
- **Offensive environment** (2026-08-17): a left-hand rail beside the board, always expanded, ranking all 32 offenses by **projected points scored** - rank, team, ppg, **core rank**, **pass share**. Three signals kept separate rather than blended, because the disagreement between them is the useful part: Clay's projected points (parsed live out of the ESPN draft-kit PDF, gated on 32-teams-and-plausible-range with a committed snapshot fallback that the UI labels), Vegas win total (hand-entered, dated), and a **fantasy core** re-scored to HBGBs settings over a fixed starter shape (QB1 + RB1-2 + WR1-3 + TE1). The fixed shape matters: summing every projected player measures committee depth, not offense quality. So does using the full player universe rather than the board's top-200 pool - that cap made five teams report **QB 0** (ARI, CLE, ATL, NYJ, PIT) because their starter sat outside the draftable cutoff. A `gap` of 6+ spots between the two ranks is colored: green where the core outranks the offense (CHI, NE, JAX - projections crediting skill players past what the team's scoring supports), amber where it's reversed (LAR, BUF, BAL, SEA, PHI, NYJ). **Pass share carries the format translation**: the 4-pt passing TD splits a score 4/6 between QB and receiver while a rushing TD hands one back all six, so with 2 FLEX and 5 bench spots, concentration beats team total. **Not a value input** - nothing here touches draft-$, edge or confidence, since the offense is already inside every player's projection. It started as a fold under Targets and that was the wrong home: the one piece of context you want *before* spending a pick was the one thing you had to remember to open. It's now a peer panel with no toggle, sticky at 216px on the left, and the board's metric columns give back 58px of padding inside the three-column band so all 13 cells still fit on one line. Rebuild: `node scripts/build-team-environment.mjs` (`--offline` skips the 5MB PDF fetch). Spec: §1.19.

The **backdrop** is football read through the existing palette rather than photographed - mown turf stripes on a 90s loop, two offset floodlight blooms, pure CSS, no asset and no extra request. Widest where you browse, almost nothing where you work. - **Roster room** (2026-08-23): a third page, `/site/rosters.html`, sitting between in-season HQ and draft day and the first one about **the other nine teams**. Each team is priced with the same league-scored projections the board runs on, then given its **optimal `QB/RB/RB/WR/WR/TE/FLEX/FLEX/K/DEF` lineup** (greedy is provably optimal because slot eligibility nests, which is what makes every marginal-gain number a comparison of true optima). Strength and weakness are read **per slot** against the other nine — "your RB2 is 9th of 10" is actionable where "7th in RB points" isn't — and **K/DEF are shown but never graded**, because a kicker rank reading as a strength is the easiest way to make the page lie. Bench surplus is measured directly: `starts on N/9` by inserting the player into each other roster and recomputing. **Trade proposals ship only where both optimal lineups rise** (+5 season points each for a 1-for-1; +8/+5 for a 2-for-1, which also requires the other side to take on more raw points — quantity for quality, the league's 2-FLEX/5-bench doctrine made literal). The first pass at the complementarity read was **wrong in an instructive way**: scoring a player by what he'd add to the other lineup just ranks him by how good he is (Jonathan Taylor "fits" all nine teams). The signal is the difference — `net = adds over there − costs over here` — and it **collapses to a constant** for everyone filling the same hole, because both legs measure against the same replacement level. Five names at an identical `+14.0` is the answer, not a bug, so the list sorts cheapest-first and says so. Owner tendencies are **parsed** out of `league-tendencies.md` (the build fails rather than shipping nine dossiers of ten) with trade counts recomputed from the raw 2020–25 transactions into a `hub/active/reachable/cold/dead` appetite chip, so the page never works up a careful offer for an owner with zero trades in six years. The search independently rediscovered a dossier trait it never reads: bwalsh89 is the league's only habitual backup-QB drafter, and the top deal against him is my RB surplus (costs me nothing) for his QB2 (costs him nothing). Rebuild: `node scripts/build-roster-room.mjs` — it **refuses to run until every roster has players**. Spec: §1.20.

### Historical-usage panel (shipped 2026-07-22)
- Replaced the placeholder `context` strip (it read "pending" for every player) with a real per-position usage panel: **recent-season shares/efficiency + a multi-year direction + a within-season trajectory**, all sample-gated. Full spec + calibration: [`plans/valuation-and-scouting.md`](plans/valuation-and-scouting.md) §1.9.
- **Stats inform, they never move value** — the projection already prices raw share and age, so usage feeds only (a) evidence, (b) the **role-stability** leg of `confidence()` (worst-of with the scouting brief's `role_stability`), (c) a one-directional **"⚑ revisit his number"** override flag. Asset value and the edge are untouched.
- Team-level denominators come from a **per-week team fingerprint** (`tm_off_snp|tm_def_snp|tm_st_snp` is identical across a team's players), so no historical roster data is needed and mid-season trades stay correct. Collided clusters and 2025-wk18's missing snap data are detected and excluded, which is why each season stamps both `g` and `share_g`.

### Accessibility
- **Accessible names on the glyph-only board controls** (2026-08-28): the draft board's take (✓) and star (☆) buttons were real buttons with `aria-pressed`, but their entire visible content is a glyph, so a screen reader had no name to read - the site had **15 `aria-pressed` and 0 `aria-label`**. Both now carry one, as does search-clear. The labels name the **player**, not just the action, because 248 buttons all announcing "Mark drafted" is a list nobody can navigate; they track state and a live-feed pick reads "drafted at pick N from the live feed". Found while auditing against the foundation's "SVG icons, never emojis as icons" rule, which read as violated and wasn't: the site has **113 non-ASCII symbols and zero emoji** - 30 are maths notation in prose (− ≥ ≈ ≤ ≠), 32 typographic connectors (→ ↳), 22 a confidence meter drawn as ●●○, and only ~29 are icons at all. Converting those to SVG would have been the wrong fix for a real problem. The foundation was clarified instead (Knowledge vault, Decisions-Log 2026-08-28), along with the responsive line: the four widths are a **test matrix**, not where media queries must sit, and the draft board is now on record as **desktop-first (1024+)** since it wraps 248 of 249 rows at 768.

### Data integrity
- **Dead board entries are filtered at build time** (2026-08-09): Ben Roethlisberger held a top-200 skill slot for 2026 despite retiring after the 2021 season, because Sleeper's own record still reads `active: true`, `status: "Active"`, `team: "PIT"`, `search_rank: 176`. An `active` check can't catch that, so `deadEntry()` requires **three independent misses** — no 2026 projection, no snap of NFL work in 2023–25, and not a rookie. Any one alone would be wrong (a holdout or suspension zeroes a projection but leaves recent stats; a rookie has no stats but is always projected, the thinnest here being 13.6 pts). Filtering happens while walking the ranked list, so the next live player takes the slot and the pool stays 200 deep; drops are **logged by name**, never silent. Demond Claiborne (RB MIN) took the vacated spot.
- **Retirement tripwire** (2026-08-09): `deadEntry()` needs a player to fail all three tests, which by construction can't see someone who retires *after* a season he played — he keeps his recent stats and only goes quiet in the next projection cycle. What every retiree does lose is the **market**: ADP goes null and Boris Chen drops him. The build now **warns** (never filters — a deep-but-active player can lack both for ordinary reasons, and silently dropping him is the mirror of the bug) on any non-rookie skill player with neither. Verified it fires on the pre-fix board (catches Roethlisberger) and is silent on the current one. A full sweep on 2026-08-09 found **no other retired players**: 0 market-blind, 0 without a 2026 projection, and the only two non-rookies with no 2025 games are Jonathon Brooks (second ACL tear, cleared for 2026) and Tyler Bass (missed 2025 with hip/groin; Buffalo's only rostered kicker), both confirmed active by current reporting.
- **Build stamps use the LOCAL date**, not `toISOString()` (UTC). `TODAY` keys the one-per-day `data/adp-history.json` snapshots, so after ~7pm Central a UTC stamp filed a second snapshot for the same real day under tomorrow's date — breaking the invariant the file documents and flattening the day-over-day delta the ADP-drift re-scout trigger reads.
- **`situation.facts` now match players by explicit `players[]` tags** on each `nfl-events.json` item (not surname substrings). Fixed the "Bijan showing Egbuka/Zac-Robinson news" class of bug across the whole board. The `nfl-daily-events` scheduled routine emits the tag going forward; existing events were backfilled.

### Waivers
- `/waivers` applies the same 3-layer model against the **FAAB market** (`data/faab-market.json` bands + rival `price_to_beat` + pressure): asset → worth → edge → PURSUE/WATCH/AVOID + rec-confidence. `waivers.json` schema + the dashboard waiver panel carry the new fields (graceful fallback for old data); the preseason board is migrated as a worked example.

### Scouting brief (evidence layer)
- `scouting_brief` = prose (analyst/coach/player sentiment + scheme fit) + `{role_stability, scheme_fit, override_flag}`, retrieval-grounded-or-null. `role_stability` feeds `confidence()` (worst-of with the historical-usage read); scheme_fit is descriptive + an override trigger. **Never moves value.**
- Filled by **`/scout`**, a resumable batch sweep, and kept current by the twice-daily re-scout drip. **Coverage is complete over the scope that has one**: all 200 skill players carry a brief with a `role_stability` the confidence model consumes. The 48 without are 16 K + 32 DEF, where no usage profile exists and scouting doesn't apply. Original design: [`plans/scouting-fork-prompt.md`](plans/scouting-fork-prompt.md).
