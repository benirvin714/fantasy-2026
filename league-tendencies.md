# League Tendencies — The HBGBs (2020–2025)

> Built from six seasons of Sleeper data: 6 drafts (900 picks), ~2,100 completed transactions, 22 trades, all brackets. Every number below was recomputed from raw data and cross-validated (FAAB totals reconcile 100% against Sleeper's `waiver_budget_used`, including 2021's FAAB trades). Raw JSON: `data/raw/`.
> Same 10 owners every season — zero turnover, so tendencies carry real predictive weight for 2026.

## League character

- **Engagement is slowly declining**: completed transactions 447 → 298 from 2020 to 2025 (−33%), driven by free-agent churn; waiver claims are stable.
- **The FAAB market is soft**: median winning bid $3–6, **67% of winning claims are uncontested**, ~21% of wins cost $0. A $10–12 bid clears virtually any routine claim. But 1–3 times a season an injury-replacement-RB frenzy clears **$30–85** (Achane $85, Guerendo $73, Ford $66, Penny $51, Woody Marks $48) — always the Tuesday after injury/breakout news.
- **The trade market is thin and concentrated**: 22 trades in six years; 17 of 22 happened in weeks 1–8. Half of all trades involve one owner (DiaperDutyDaddy). Stars do move here (Chase ×2, Kelce, Saquon, CeeDee, Adams, Henry, Mahomes).
- **K/DEF market sleeps until round 13**: 52 of 59 drafted kickers and 47 of 57 defenses went in the last three rounds. Nobody has ever followed an early K/DEF pick with a run.
- **QB market is late by modern standards**: 2–7 QBs gone through round 5; exactly one elite QB goes round 2–3 each year, then a cliff to rounds 5–7. TE has **never had a positional run** in 900 picks — mid TEs can always be waited on one more round.

## Owner dossiers

### mfkr (commissioner, roster 1)
- **Draft:** RB in round 1 all six drafts (a lock). Earliest QB drafter — first QB avg rd 4.7, rounds 3–5 in each of the last four (Allen, Hurts ×2, Daniels). TE rounds 3–5 most years. Reaches for hyped rookies (CEH #6 overall, Najee rd2, Nabers rd4).
- **FAAB:** early-season burner — spent 61–85% of budget by week 4 in five of six seasons; biggest bid $85 Achane (2023, two days after the 4-TD game, $34 over next bid). 2025 was his first restrained year.
- **Trades:** basically none (2 in six years).
- **Exploit:** he's your main competition for an elite QB — beat him to round 3–4 or forget it. From ~week 8 on he's usually broke: win contested claims against him for $5–10. His round 1 is always an RB, so a WR you want at his slot is safe. Don't bother sending trade offers.

### DiaperDutyDaddy (roster 2)
- **Draft:** most RB-heavy owner (3.17 RB in rounds 1–6; rd1 RB in 5 of 6). QB regime change: rounds 8–11 in 2020–22 → 6 → 4 → **Josh Allen at pick 11 in 2025**. Expect him to pay up for QB again.
- **FAAB:** budget sits early, panic-bids late ($55 Kendre Miller week 15, $38 over next bid; $38 SF DEF week 14).
- **Trades:** **the league's trade hub — party to 11 of 22 league trades**, deals in-season including deadline week, has moved real stars.
- **Exploit:** best trade partner in the league; send him offers before touching waivers. Early season his FAAB is dormant; late season he's in every frenzy. Worst current roster — he'll be rebuilding-adjacent and reachable.

### ENOTS (roster 3)
- **Draft:** converted QB-punter — first QB rounds 12–13 in three of the last four. WR-lean early, TE always rounds 6–10.
- **FAAB:** highest volume in the league (36 adds/season), empties the budget every year ($94–100 spent all six seasons), **overbids contested claims by +$7.3 on average** ($48 Woody Marks was $22 over next). Stays aggressive into fantasy playoffs.
- **Trades:** rare (4) but reachable.
- **Exploit:** never fear him in QB/TE decisions after rd 5. If you both want an injury-replacement RB, expect to need $40+ — or price-enforce him, since he wins by wide margins. Two titles at .500 overall; he peaks in playoffs, don't let his seed fool you.

### pauldag (roster 4)
- **Draft:** most extreme QB-punter — never before rd 5 in six drafts, **drafted ZERO QBs in 2025**. WR-leaning early. K rd 14–15 every year.
- **FAAB:** biggest hoarder — spent **$0 in 2022 and 2025**, $11 in 2024. But his rare bids are haymakers from a full wallet ($66 Ford, $51 Penny — his only bids those entire years).
- **Trades:** 1 in six years.
- **Exploit:** never price-adjust for him on routine claims — he grabs everything as post-clear free agency instead. EXCEPT in an RB frenzy, where his full wallet can snipe. QBs and K/DEF always fall past him. Not a trade outlet.

### Stipe (roster 5)
- **Draft:** WR-anchored — rd2 was a WR in 5 of 6 drafts, WR rd1 in 3 of the last 4. Newly aggressive on young TEs (McBride rd3 in 2025) — now a competitor for your TE habit.
- **FAAB:** passive price-taker; six-year max bid $29, 2025 max bid $9; ends most years with $25–65 unspent.
- **Trades:** none since 2021.
- **Exploit:** $12–15 beats him for almost anything. RBs are safe at his early turns; WRs are not. Declining team (4-10, #9 points in 2025).

### DadKing (roster 6)
- **Draft:** the league metronome — QB rds 3–7 (pounces rd3 when Mahomes/Lamar-tier falls), TE rds 4–6, K rd 13–14 and DEF rd 14–15 in **all six drafts**. Fully forecastable.
- **FAAB:** disciplined mid-band; **six-year maximum bid $25** — he has never thrown a haymaker. Best price discipline in the league (+$4.9 avg winning margin).
- **Trades:** 6, reachable (Mahomes deadline deal, the Chase deal).
- **Exploit:** bid $26+ and you take any player from him — guaranteed on six years of data. If an elite QB slides toward his rd3 slot, beat him there. No longer a free win — improving four straight years and closed 2025 one game out.

### bwalsh89 (roster 7)
- **Draft:** TE-early since 2021 (rds 2–5 in four of six) — **your direct competitor in the elite-TE window**. The league's only habitual backup-QB drafter (2 QBs in 5 of 6 drafts). Getting rookie-aggressive (Jeanty rd2).
- **FAAB:** least active (18.7 adds/season) but highest bid median ($10.5) — bids rarely and big. Author of the worst overpay in league history ($56 Allen Robinson vs next bid $14).
- **Trades:** 3, all since 2024 — waking up.
- **Exploit:** ignores waivers for weeks — his roster back-end goes stale and he won't contest routine claims; but never assume you can beat him cheap on a player he's decided he wants. If you plan to stream QBs, grab your QB2 before his last-round backup-QB habit thins the pool.

### StoneBone69 (roster 8)
- **Draft:** RB-heavy hoover (3.17 RB rounds 1–6), double-punts QB+TE (QB rd7+ in 5 of 6 — once rd15; TE avg rd 8.2, latest in league). Drafts exactly one QB every year.
- **FAAB:** the volume sprayer — 136 failed bids (2.4× the next owner), wins on persistence not price ($1–7 typical), max ever $45. The only owner to ever trade FOR FAAB.
- **Trades:** 9 — second-most; the DiaperDutyDaddy–StoneBone69 channel (4 trades) sets the league's market.
- **Exploit:** ~$10–12 clears him on routine players. **Drop players carefully — he scoops everything.** He'll hoover mid-round RBs: if an RB you want is near his rd3–6 pick, take it a slot early. Top-2 points three straight years — the most consistent current threat.

### Sladsous (roster 9)
- **Draft:** RB-RB opener (rd1 RB 5 of 6, rd2 RB 4 of 6). Elite-TE early 2020–22, total punt since. QB rounds 4–5, favors rushing QBs (Lamar ×2, Hurts). **The league's most rookie-averse owner** — zero rookies before rd9 in six years.
- **FAAB:** hoard-then-burn — underbids through ~week 8, then the single most dangerous late-season frenzy bidder ($73 Guerendo week 13, $32 over next).
- **Trades:** **ZERO in six seasons.** Do not waste time on offers.
- **Exploit:** book his first two picks as RBs; rookies and mid TEs sail past him. Beat him cheap on waivers before November; assume he goes big in any late frenzy. Reigning champ with the #1 offense — the team to beat.

### ThatWasButtery — you (roster 10). Self-scout.
- **History:** 44-38, 3 playoff trips, 0 titles, 2025 runner-up. Boom-bust: #1 points in 2020 (semis exit — bad luck), points rank 6th–9th four straight years 2021–24 (a genuine scoring problem, not luck), #4 points in 2025. **The fix is raising baseline weekly scoring; 2025's finals run outran the points rank.**

## The playbook (2026)

1. **Draft:** let someone else take the first K/DEF for once — rounds 14–15. Elite TE only at value, and expect bwalsh89 (and now Stipe) to compete for it. mfkr and DiaperDutyDaddy are the QB-sniping threats at rds 2–4; everyone else lets QBs fall.
2. **FAAB pricing:** routine adds $1–12 (67% of claims go uncontested). DadKing capitulates at $26. Stipe at ~$15. StoneBone69 needs ~$10–12 but files claims on everything. ENOTS and frenzies need $40–85 — commit $60+ or keep the powder. pauldag and late-season Sladsous can haymaker from full wallets.
3. **Add volume:** be more active in weeks 5–9 when the league's budgets thin — your historical under-activity there is a free edge.
4. **Trades:** work DiaperDutyDaddy and StoneBone69 early (September–October); DadKing is reachable; Sladsous/pauldag/Stipe/mfkr are dead ends. After week 8, only DiaperDutyDaddy reliably transacts. Consolidate via 2-for-1s (see league-profile.md — the format makes it +EV).
5. **Drops:** anything you cut, StoneBone69 may scoop — time drops after waivers clear when it matters.
