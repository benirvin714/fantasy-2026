2. **ChrisHasAfib (3-0) gained the most from the injury wave.** Addison's 36% share and Washington's sustained 27% are target volume, the thing full PPR pays for twice, and both sit on one roster. Expect him to hold rather than sell either while Jefferson is day-to-day.

4. **Gordon is less of a prize here than in the half-PPR leagues.** Three targets on 20 touches is a rushing role, and in full PPR a carry-and-goal-line back loses ground to pass-catchers. Denver's split works the other way: Harvey's receiving work, which is nDabb's, gains relative to Dobbins's carries (haleymegert's).
5. **sierragrace (0-3) lost Etienne and got a no-touchdown night from Hurts.** With Dalton Schultz also losing volume if Collins returns, the roster's depth is thinning at the wrong time; he is a candidate to trade for immediate points.
6. **At 84 startable RB/WR slots, the wire is the thinnest of the three leagues.** With every owner still at 100 FAAB and five rosters listing RB as a weak spot and two more (brobertson2, sierragrace) just losing a starting back, a lead back like Gordon or Braelon Allen should draw more bidders here than anywhere else.
