## So what for this league

1. **JSN at ADP ~5 is format-correct here.** Elite WRs are worth *more* than consensus in this league (elite-vs-free gap is the only durable edge), and his 21+ ppg profile survives the half-PPR haircut because it's yardage-driven (1,793 yards), not catch-padded. If you pick top-3, he's a legitimate selection — and your rivals know you want him.
2. **A.J. Brown at #16 (round 2) is a fair-to-rich price in half-PPR** — the profile is yardage/TD-heavy which travels well, but a Vrabel offense's pass volume is the risk. The real league angle: **Drake Maye's ceiling rise is a konami-code signal** — rushing QBs score at RB rates here, and ENOTS (Maye's 2025 owner) is a round-12 QB-punter who won't pay up. If Maye's camp reports stay hot, he's a rounds 6–8 target that nobody in this room will contest except mfkr/DiaperDutyDaddy.
3. **Gainwell at ADP 100 is a market fade for this league.** His value is receptions (73 catches), worth exactly half of full-PPR hype at 0.5/rec. Irving-at-cost is the right side of that backfield — and if Irving's camp health wobbles, the *frenzy math* from league-tendencies.md applies: ambiguous-backfield resolution is what clears $60+ in-season, not draft capital now.
4. **Judkins is a conviction buy at whatever discount the leg injury leaves.** Expected Week 1, and Monken's Ravens-style scheme (2nd-highest run-action rate) is the best RB ecosystem transplant of the cycle. Expect bwalsh89 (rookie-aggressive, drafted him last year) to be the competition.
5. **Three unresolved QB battles (CLE, MIN, ATL) are a subsidy for this league's late-QB market.** With 10 starters and QB13–16 free all season, battle losers cost nothing and battle winners are September waiver adds — do not spend draft picks resolving these; let the wire tell you.
6. **ARI ecosystem caution lands on three rivals** (McBride–Stipe, Harrison–StoneBone69, Benson–DiaperDutyDaddy), not on you. If Stipe's newly aggressive early-TE habit meets a LaFleur-rebuild discount on McBride, let him have it — mid-TE is this format's trap anyway.
7. **Kamara/Conner "value" is fake value here.** Aging RB depth is exactly the archetype this league replaces for free on waivers (~7–8 ppg). DiaperDutyDaddy held Kamara in 2025; if he re-drafts the profile, his roster stays beatable.
8. **SF stack watch**: Evans + Kittle + Pearsall + Purdy behind an unsettled left guard. The offense is loaded but the protection question is real — monitor camp before paying for the stack; Kittle remains the one TE profile (yardage-heavy, not reception-dependent) that holds value in half-PPR.
9. **Seattle won the Super Bowl and camp opened today** — champion-team ADP inflation is a known market bias; JSN earns it, but be skeptical of secondary Seahawks (Charbonnet/Emanuel Wilson types) riding the halo at cost.
10. **Calendar**: all 32 camps open by July 28. The next brief after camps open will carry the first real depth-chart signal; until then, ADP movement is the only market that matters.
